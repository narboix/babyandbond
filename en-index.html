Baby & Bond

Play and bond with your baby

Baby & Bond helps mothers and fathers interact with their baby, build a bond and help their baby be happy. Every day it suggests simple ideas to play, watch and be together, matched to your baby’s exact stage from 0 to 18 months.

What it offers

  • Every day, three ideas to play with your baby from different themes: bonding, massage, sensory, motor, communication and autonomy.
  • 36 two-week stages, from 0 to 18 months.
  • Corrected age for premature babies and support for twins.
  • A guide to come back to: principles, baby’s cues, baby massage, safety and screens, and sources of information.
  • Available in English, español, català and français.
  • No accounts, no ads and no data collection.

Free to try, one payment to unlock everything

Free: one of the three daily ideas, the current stage, the safety guide and up to 3 saved activities. With a one-time purchase, no subscription, you unlock every stage, all three daily ideas, the full guide and unlimited saved activities.

Baby & Bond is a play and bonding app. It isn’t a pediatric app and doesn’t assess development: for health or development questions, talk to your pediatrician.
