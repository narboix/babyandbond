Baby & Bond Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short

Baby & Bond doesn’t collect, share or sell your data. You don’t need an account, the app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn’t connect to any server to work.

Who is responsible

Nivard Arboix

Contact: aibrickcode@gmail.com

What information the app uses

To work, the app asks for or stores:

  • Your baby’s name or names (one or two, if they are twins).
  • Their birth date and, if they were born early, the due date.
  • The activities you save as favorites.
  • The language you choose inside the app.

Where it is stored

All of this information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the app’s publisher or to any third party, and we can’t see it. If you have a backup of your device turned on (for example, in iCloud), the app’s data may be part of that backup; Apple manages that according to your settings.

How to delete your data

In Settings, the “Delete all data” option removes your baby’s profile and the saved activities from the device. They are also deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The app offers a single in-app purchase that unlocks all content. Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store: we don’t receive or store your payment information. The app only checks on your device, through Apple’s system, whether the purchase is active. You can read how Apple handles your data at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Permissions

The app doesn’t ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or notifications.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party services, libraries or ads that collect data.

Children

Baby & Bond is intended for parents and caregivers. It isn’t directed at children. A baby’s details are entered by an adult and stay on their device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to aibrickcode@gmail.com.
