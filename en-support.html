Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock all the content?

Tap any idea with a lock, or “Unlock everything” in Settings, and complete the one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you switch iPhones or reinstall the app, open Settings and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I change the app language?

In Settings, under Language. You can choose English, español, català or français without changing your iPhone’s language.

My baby was born prematurely

When you create the profile you can enter the due date and the app will use the corrected age. You can change it in Settings by tapping your baby’s profile.

What if I have twins?

You can enter two names in the profile.

How do I delete my baby’s data?

In Settings, “Delete all data” removes the profile and the saved activities from your device.

My baby is older than 18 months

The guide covers 0 to 18 months. After that, the Today screen tells you so; the stages remain available in the Two weeks tab.

Is this a medical app?

No. Baby & Bond is a play and bonding app: it isn’t pediatric and doesn’t assess development. For health or development questions, talk to your pediatrician.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
